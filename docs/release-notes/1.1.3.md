# OCTET Draw - 1.1.3 - Patch notes

## Highlights

* Edit structures in Word on the Mac and in LibreOffice: Double-click a structure in Word and it should open in Octet.
   * In LibreOffice on Windows and Mac, choose Insert > Octet Drawing, or double-click a drawing to edit it.
   * This is a hard implementation, so please feedback if you have more bugs!
* Peptides and oligos: They can be drawn from a sequence or HELM, with disulfide and lactam bridges, cyclic chains, D- and non-natural amino acids, protecting groups, modified nucleotides and your own monomers. Copy them back out as HELM or FASTA.
* Stereochemistry tools: Convert between Fischer projections and wedges, invert one stereocentre, redraw stereo bonds, and show D/L and α/β. Octet flags wedges that set nothing.
* Pen tablets and touch screens: The app should work on your touch screen machines as you’d expect.
* Measure, angles, and dihedrals: Lengths, angles and dihedrals between atoms or any point on the page, with real 3D values on a flat drawing.
* LaTeX. Copy structures and whole reaction schemes as chemfig code.
* NMReDATA. Saving and and open NMR assignments has been added. They can be swapped back and forth with MestReNova (tested).
* Jablonski diagrams. Pick one from the Photochemistry templates, and edit it on the page.
* Work from 43 of your GitHub issues.

Thank you all so much, 2.5 weeks in and It’s getting good!
Take care, HUGE new features next Friday - Sam

## Drawing

* The Pen tool a new tool that functions as it does on Photoshop and CD. Basically makes arrows easier to draw.
* The Measure tool can measure lengths, angels and dihedral angles. The tool locks onto atoms and a dotted line drags out behind your selection.
* A lot of bug fixes
   * A template fuses to the atom or bond you pick on the template. (#149)
   * Curved arrows are now attached to atoms, bonds, or lone pairs when you move the entire structure. (#143)
   * A text box dragged onto a reaction arrow will now become the conditions (it snaps onto the arrow). There is a ‘gap slider’ to set the space between the arrow and the text. (#144)
   * You can now hold shift to drag a selection in a straight line. If you hold option, it will swap the box and lasso selection tools akin to CD. (#169)
   * The Arrow menu now has a Large, Medium and Small option at the top.
   * For a double bond, the Actions panel gives you an option to place it inside or outside of the ring / on the LHS / RHS of the molecule.
   * Templates menu has been organised a lot better.
   * Text boxes can now be framed: click one with the Box tool, or use Add frame in the menu. A single word can be highlighted, like a bond.

## Charges

* Charges can now be dragged away from an atom, and given a different colour and size. (#164)
* Click empty space with the Symbols tool to drop a free charge, radical, lone pair or δ+ / δ− anywhere. (#164)
* Nu, E, LG and the other stand-in labels take a charge too: point at one and press + or −. (#164)
* Charges can be circled, and free charges go from +8 to −8. (#60)
* Radical cations and anions are now drawn as one symbol (•+ and •−). (#163)
* A + or 2− typed next to an ion now counts as a charge on the ion, so Clean Up Reaction moves them together. (#164)

## Labels and names

* Labels now read the way chemists write them: t-Bu, OtBu, Bpin, 9-BBN, allyl, 4-ClC6H4, 3,5-(CF3)2C6H3, CD3 and BF3K all give the right formula and mass.
   * P(O)(OEt)2, C(O)OMe and S(O)2Me keep the double bonds.
   * Labels written backwards (BocNH, MeO2S, F3C) read from the right atom.
   * Condensed formulas like C6H5, OC2H5 and COOC2H5 read as the group you mean. (#142, #146)
* About 165 new nicknames (Morph, 2-Py, Pbf, Bcat and loads more), and you can now make your own with Structure > Define Nickname. (#146)
* Ar is now a generic group like R and X (1.1.2 read it as phenyl, sorry). Alkyl, PG, EWG and Het are stand-ins too.
* Structure to Name gives r and s for pseudoasymmetric centres, names cages drawn in 3D, and has a Stop button for big structures. (#82, #155)
* Type \oplus or \ominus for ⊕ or ⊖. (#124)

## Stereochemistry (#145, #150)

* New in the Structure menu: Invert Configuration Here, Convert to Fischer Projection, Convert to Wedge Drawing and Redraw Stereo Bonds.
* D/L, steroid α/β and marks on undefined stereocentres can be switched on in Settings > Drawing > Stereochemistry.
* Octet flags wedges that set nothing, and Fix redraws them. Ambiguous allenes and unclear E/Z bonds get flagged too.

## Peptides, DNA and RNA (#27)

* Bridges from HELM are drawn as brackets over the backbone, and two chains (e.g. insulin) go one above the other. Cyclic peptides close with a bracket below.
* D-amino acids (A[dF]C), 14 non-natural amino acids, and end groups like Boc-, Fmoc- and -OMe.
* Modified nucleotides ([mA], [fU], [moeG]) and phosphorothioates with *.
* Make your own monomers, and import or export HELM monomer libraries.
* Switch a document's sequences between atoms and residue labels.
* Click a chain with the Sequence tool to edit the sequence in place.
* Paste a bare sequence like ATGCATGC and the name field opens with it.
* ATP, NAD and CoA drawn as atoms now stay small molecules.

## Tables and the reagent table

* Bold, italic, underline, subscript and superscript now work on the characters in any table cell (⌘B, ⌘I etc.).
* You can type in any reagent table cell, add your own rows, and delete any row or part of it.
* Reagent tables snap like everything else and stay where you put them. Attach to reaction puts one back.
* Make Static turns a reagent table into plain text and lines (⌘Z brings it back).
* Amounts show one decimal from 10 up (146.5 mg). A formula, yield or compound number you type carries through to the table and the drawing.
* The risk assessment now finds hazards for reagents typed as text, and knows 36 more bench reagents offline (KHSO5, RuCl3, CAN, AgOTf, NaBH(OAc)3 etc.).

## Properties, hazards and suppliers

* Find Suppliers lists every vendor PubChem has for a structure, with country and catalogue number. Star the ones you use.
* Hazards now has a separate tab next to Properties.
* Properties shows H-bond donors, H-bond acceptors and rings, and Decimals now sets the places for every calculated value. (#152, #77)
* Hide property names shows a live value with no labels, and it stays live. (#77)

## Charts and spectra

* Energy diagrams: pick Energy diagram from the Photochemistry templates to draw a reaction energy profile. Paste the numbers straight from a spreadsheet, or type over a level on the page.
* Sensitivity screens: Object > Add Chart > Sensitivity Screen… draws a Glorius screen with the ten standard conditions.
* Jablonski diagrams can be edited: drag levels and transitions, and add singlets and triplets. (#43)
* Better ¹³C predictions around peroxides. (#83)

## Data, notes and long text

* Data fields can be attached to structures, shown on the page, and kept through SDF and ChemDraw files. (#68)
* Notes can go on the drawing, or on a text, arrow, shape, cycle or group, in a Notes panel. ChemDraw annotations open there too. (#101, #102)
* A text box now holds up to 20,000 characters, so a whole procedure with the characterisation data fits.

## Files and formats

* Export an ELN archive, and RDF and SDF files with your reagent table data. (#117)
* Export every page of a document as one PDF. (#156)
* --convert now converts files or folders to any format Export does, e.g. --convert figures --to pdf --width 89mm. (#91)
* ChemDraw round trips: Ar and other generic labels stay labels, and posters keep the same page size.
* Smaller bits: paste CIF text, add a comment to a document, File > Open Example, Home tiles show titles, and .oct files have an Octet icon.

## Office and LibreOffice

* On Windows, pictures in Word, PowerPoint and Excel are now smooth at every zoom with no missing bond lines. Export as EMF gives the same picture. (#166, #167)
* In Word on the Mac, the top and right edges of a picture are no longer clipped. (#171)
* Double-click to edit in Word on the Mac is now on by default (Settings > General > Word to turn it off).
* LibreOffice: click Add to LibreOffice in Settings > General first. (#48)

## Pen tablets and touch screens (#93)

* A pen draws like the mouse. The eraser end erases and the side button opens the Actions menu.
* One finger draws, two fingers move and zoom, and touch and hold opens the menu.
* After you use a pen, a finger only moves and zooms, so your hand can rest on the screen.
* Tools and menus get bigger when a tablet's keyboard is off.

## Windows, Mac and Linux

* Window > Tile Side by Side, Tile Top to Bottom and Cascade on Windows and Linux. (#73)
* Window > Zoom and Bring All to Front on the Mac. (#73)
* On Linux, cursors fit scaled displays, and the AppImage uses the system's WebKitGTK when it can, so drawing uses the graphics card. (#165, #37)

## Speed

* Dragging in a big drawing is about a third faster, most of all on a page with reagent tables. (#37)
* Deleting atoms from a very large drawing takes about 40% less time.

## Colours

* Pinned colour rows, a full palette, and Save to colours.

## Fixes

* Box and lasso now select only the atoms inside them. (#162)
* The Eraser takes one line off a double or triple bond, as in CD. (#140)
* Undo straight after opening a drawing no longer wipes it.
* Dropping a spectrum on Home asks before it replaces unsaved work.
* Closing or minimising the Mass spec panel no longer moves the drawing. (#161)
* A CDXML file saved as .oct keeps the ChemDraw stereo when saved as CDXML again.
* The round Feedback button can be turned off in Settings > Pro.
