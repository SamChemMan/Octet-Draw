# Octet Draw 1.1.1

This update fixes problems you reported and adds features you asked for.

## Highlights

- The Sequence tool draws peptides, RNA and DNA.
- Octet draws ferrocene and other metallocenes, and predicts their NMR shifts.
- Name to Structure reads 88,000 more names and starts faster.
- On a chair, new bonds are axial or equatorial.
- ChemDraw files open with their Greek letters, pictures and labels.
- On Windows and Linux, Ctrl-click and Ctrl-drag do the same as in ChemDraw.

## New

- Sequence tool: click on the page, then type the one-letter codes.
- Choose the ends below the codes, or type them, as in Ac-ACD-NH2 or pACGU.
- Choose Residue labels to show each residue as a label, as in H–Ala–Cys–OH.
- Structure > Expand Label and Contract Label change a residue between atoms and a label.
- Type \delta to write δ, as in LaTeX. Text > Character Map has more symbols. The idea is from Prof. Stefan Schramm.
- Superscript (⌘= or Ctrl+=) and Subscript (⌘- or Ctrl+-) work in atom labels, for example Fe(III).
- Draw a bond from a metal to the centre of a ring, as in ferrocene.
- On a chair, the first new bond on an atom is axial. The second is equatorial.
- New templates: a stereocentre with wedge and hash bonds, and a Fischer projection.
- Convert Structure to Name reads the stereochemistry of a chair.
- Clean Up moves labels off bonds and other labels.
- Align and Distribute line up the atoms you select.
- No-go, dipole and equilibrium arrows have more head styles.
- Right-click an arrow, a catalytic cycle or an atom label to change it.
- Scroll bars, middle-button drag and Shift-scroll move the view.
- Centre in Window (⌥⌘/ or Alt+Ctrl+/) is in the View menu and the Actions panel.
- Experiment pages have Reaction scheme and Procedure boxes, roman numerals and one reagent on each row.
- On Home, select many drawings to discard them or move them to a folder.

## NMR

- Octet predicts ¹H and ¹³C shifts for ferrocene, (benzene)Cr(CO)₃ and similar complexes.
- Octet predicts a shift for every atom. It marks less accurate shifts as Rough.
- Spectra on the page have the same splitting as the Spectrum panel.
- Choose Labels > Auto, Horizontal or Vertical. Label lines do not cross.
- You can edit all the text of a spectrum. Drag a handle to change its size.
- Shift numbers beside atoms do not touch bonds or labels.
- Shift numbers in ChemDraw files open.

## Name to Structure

- It reads 88,000 more names and CAS numbers from the FDA UNII list. Try 129-64-6.
- It starts faster and uses less memory.
- It reads endo, exo, syn and anti.
- It draws crowded natural products, such as aconitine, with no warnings.

## ChemDraw and Word

- Greek letters in ChemDraw files, such as hν, open correctly.
- Pictures in ChemDraw files from Windows open.
- Accented letters and ° are correct in other programs.
- Copy and Export send ChemDraw only what you select.
- Octet saves subscripts in conditions, such as Pd(PPh₃)₄.
- Labels in MOL files, such as OMe, open as labels.
- On Windows, labels in pictures you paste into Word are text.
- Copied pictures have less empty space around the drawing.
- Octet shows a message when it cannot read the clipboard.
- Octet shows a message when ChemDraw removed Octet data from a file.

## Windows and Linux

- Ctrl-click adds to the selection. Ctrl-drag copies it, as in ChemDraw.
- Home opens again when you close a drawing.
- On Windows, the menu bar stays when you close another window.
- On Linux, structures paste into Inkscape at the correct size.
- On Linux, tips use less graphics power.

## Fixes

- The 1.0 selection colour is back. Change it in Settings > General > Selection.
- The Properties panel marks predicted values, such as boiling point and logP. So do Copy and Add to drawing.
- File > New always opens a new drawing.
- On the Mac, Octet opens again after an update.
- Labels such as N(CH3)2, CH2N3, SO2Me and CONHMe give the correct structure.
- ODMT changes to DMTO when its bond is on the right.
- Azide, diazo, isocyanate and sulfone templates have the correct bonds.
- Bold and wedge bonds on chair and Haworth rings do not cause stereo warnings.
- Catalytic cycle names do not overlap. Long names wrap.
- In conditions, Pd(0)L₂ shows its subscript.
- The reagent table does not include N₂, Ar and air.
- In a wide reaction, the starting materials stay beside their arrow.
- Clean Up gives the same result each time.
- All nucleobase templates face the same way.
- Add to drawing puts items in an empty area.
- Settings > Document > Page edges has All sizes, Page only and None.
- All sections of the Actions panel open and close in the same way.
- On experiment pages, the pointer is a text cursor over the details.
- The Template button shows a books icon for large templates.
- In ChemDraw files, Ac and Pr labels open as acetyl and propyl.
- Expand Label works on a contracted label after you open the .oct file again.
- A drawing with a shadowed box exports, copies and prints at the correct size.
