# Octet Draw 1.1.1

This update fixes problems you reported in 1.1.0 and adds features you asked for.

## Highlights

- Draw peptides, RNA and DNA with the new Sequence tool.
- Draw ferrocene and other metallocenes. Octet names them and predicts their NMR shifts.
- Name to Structure knows 88,000 more compounds and is ready sooner.
- Type \delta to get δ.
- ChemDraw files keep their Greek letters and pictures.
- On Windows and Linux, Ctrl-click and Ctrl-drag work as in ChemDraw.

## New

- Use the Sequence tool to draw peptides, RNA and DNA. Click where the structure goes, then type the one-letter codes.
- Below the codes, choose the ends: N-acetyl and C-amide, or 5′- and 3′-phosphate. You can also type them, as in Ac-ACD-NH2 or pACGU.
- Choose Residue labels to draw each residue as a label, as in H–Ala–Cys–OH. Formulas and names still read every atom. Structure > Expand Label draws a residue as atoms, and Contract Label makes a residue a label again.
- Type \delta to get δ, as in LaTeX. Text > Character Map has more signs. The idea is from Prof. Stefan Schramm.
- Superscript (⌘= or Ctrl+=) and Subscript (⌘- or Ctrl+-) work in atom labels, for example Fe(III).
- Metallocenes: draw a bond from a metal to the centre of a ring, as in ferrocene.
- Chair cyclohexane: a new bond is axial. The next bond on that atom is equatorial.
- Stereocentre templates: a carbon with wedge and hash bonds, and a Fischer projection.
- Convert Structure to Name reads the stereochemistry of a chair.
- Clean Up moves labels off bonds and other labels.
- Align and Distribute line up the atoms you select.
- No-go, dipole and equilibrium arrows have more head styles.
- Right-click an arrow, a catalytic cycle or an atom label to change it.
- Scroll bars, middle-button drag and Shift-scroll move you around the drawing.
- Centre in window (⌥⌘/ or Alt+Ctrl+/) is in the Actions panel and the View menu.
- Experiment pages: Reaction scheme and Procedure boxes, roman numerals and one reagent on each row.
- Home: select many drawings, then discard them or move them to a folder.

## NMR

- Ferrocene, (benzene)Cr(CO)3 and similar complexes get ¹H and ¹³C shifts.
- Every atom gets a shift. A shift with no close match shows as Rough.
- A spectrum in the drawing shows the same splitting as the Spectrum panel.
- Choose Labels > Auto, Horizontal or Vertical. Label lines do not cross.
- Edit all the text of a spectrum. Drag a handle to change its size.
- Shift numbers beside atoms do not touch bonds or labels.
- Shift numbers from ChemDraw predictions open.

## Name to Structure

- It knows 88,000 more compounds and CAS numbers, from the FDA's UNII list. Try 129-64-6.
- It is ready sooner after Octet opens and uses less memory.
- It reads endo, exo, syn and anti.
- It draws crowded natural products, such as aconitine, with no warnings.

## ChemDraw and Word

- Greek letters in ChemDraw files open correctly: hν, not hn.
- Pictures in ChemDraw files from Windows open.
- Accented letters and ° are correct in other programs.
- Copy and Export give ChemDraw only what you select.
- Subscripts in conditions, such as Pd(PPh₃)₄, are saved.
- Labels in MOL files, such as OMe, open as labels.
- On Windows, labels in pictures pasted into Word are real text.
- Copied pictures fit the drawing more closely.
- Octet tells you when it cannot read the clipboard.
- Octet tells you when ChemDraw removed Octet's extras from a file.

## Windows and Linux

- Ctrl-click adds to the selection. Ctrl-drag copies it, as in ChemDraw.
- Home shows again when you close a drawing.
- On Linux, structures paste into Inkscape at their true size.
- On Linux, the pulse in the tips uses less graphics power.

## Fixes

- The 1.0 selection colour is back. Change it in Settings > General > Selection.
- File > New always opens a new drawing.
- On the Mac, Octet opens again after an update.
- Labels such as N(CH3)2, CH2N3, SO2Me and CONHMe give the correct structure.
- ODMT shows as DMTO when its bond comes from the right.
- Azide, diazo, isocyanate and sulfone templates have the correct bonds.
- Bold and wedge bonds on chair and Haworth rings give no stereo warnings.
- Catalytic cycle names do not overlap. Long names wrap.
- In conditions, Pd(0)L₂ gets its subscript.
- The reagent table ignores N2, Ar and air.
- A wide reaction keeps its starting materials beside their arrow.
- Clean Up gives the same result each time.
- The nucleobase templates all face the same way.
- Add to drawing puts items in free space.
- Settings > Document > Page edges: All sizes, Page only or None.
- All Actions panel sections fold in the same way.
- Experiment pages: a text cursor shows over the details.
- The Template button shows a books icon for large templates.
- An Ac or Pr label in a ChemDraw file opens as acetyl or propyl, not as an element.
- A label made with Contract Label keeps its atoms in .oct files. Expand Label works after you open the file again.
- A drawing with a shadowed box exports, copies and prints at its true size. Before, Export said it was too large.
