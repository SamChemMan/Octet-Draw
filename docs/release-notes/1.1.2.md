# OCTET Draw - 1.1.2 - Patch notes

- Integration with the office 365 4 (haha) suite is complete. The same can be said for apple’s creative suite, and LibreOffice. It works better than ChemDraw, see further below

## Spectra and NMR

- The spectrum panel and the Mass spec panel controls (at the top right) have been refined and this is now consistent with how windows and MacOS work (Minimise, Full screen and Close)
- When you open a spectrum, the Actions panel will slide out the way. When you open the Actions panel, The same will happen to your spectrum.
- If you close a spectrum by mistake, you can CMD+Z (undo) before changing the drawing and the spectrum will come back.
- The new MS panel can be opened via Structure > Show Mass Spec Analysis, or click Mass spec analysis in the Actions panel.
- ¹H and ¹³C spectra show the splitting from fluorine and phosphorus atoms in your molecule.
- To show or hide the ¹H couplings in a ¹⁹F or ³¹P spectrum, click Decouple ¹H. ¹⁹F spectra show them at first. ³¹P spectra do not.
- Octet opens files of measured ¹⁹F and ³¹P spectra. If a file does not give the nucleus, Octet will try to predict it based on the resonance frequency.
- Predicted ¹H and ¹³C shifts are more accurate near sulfonyl groups, in heteroaromatic rings and in bridged bicyclic rings.
- When you click Details in a ¹H spectrum, the Quality column shows Rough for OH, NH and SH shifts.
- Solvent effects now considered

## Templates

- Eight new clip art sets: Lab equipment, Biomolecules, Cells, BioArt, Anatomy, Organisms, Bugs and Symbols.
- Seven new structure sets: Side chains, Peptides, DNA and RNA, Supramolecules, Polyhedra, Carbon and Photochemistry.
- The Polyhedra set has 14 coordination polyhedra, from the trigonal bipyramid to the icosahedron. Each corner is a position for a ligand. (Nitschke group goes wild).
- The Carbon set has C60 and C70. (Nitschke group goes wild).
- The Lab set has 31 fresh items, such as a three-neck flask, a Dean-Stark trap, a Schlenk line and a photoreactor.
- In the template library, the menus are now laid out properly; search in templates is also more refined

## Gels, plasmid maps and membranes

Please feedback on this - I am no biochemist!

- The Gel tool will draw a gel of five lanes. The first lane, M, has a protein ladder.
- To add a band, click a lane with the Gel tool. To change the size of a band, drag the band.
- To type the sizes of the bands for a lane, click the lane and choose Add bands… in the Actions panel.
- To change the largest and the smallest size of the gel, choose Set the range… in the Actions panel. Each band keeps its size.
- When you click the page with the Plasmid map tool, Octet draws a ring of 5000 bp. Base pair 1 is at the top.
- To add a region or a marker to a map, choose Add a region… or Add a marker… in the Actions panel.
- To change a lane name, a region or a marker, double-click it. To give a region or a marker its own colour, right-click it.
- To draw a membrane, hold the Shape tool and choose Membrane. Then drag on the page.
- To change a line, an arc or a pen curve into a membrane, select it. Then choose Membrane from Line in the Actions panel.
- When you drag the end of a membrane, Octet adds lipids. The lipids stay the same size

## Stoichiometry table

- When a scheme has two or more arrows, Structure > Analyse Stoichiometry makes one table for each step. This is a fix from last update.
- When you input the Measured mass of a product, Octet sets the amount of that compound in the next step automatically.
- Each reactant column has a role: Reactant, Catalyst, Solvent etc. You can, of course change this (choose a role in the Actions panel).
- In a new table, Octet gives the Solvent role to each common solvent above or below the arrow, such as THF, CHCl3, etc.
- For a catalyst, the Equivalents row shows mol%. Type 5 or 5% to set 5 mol%.
- For a solvent, the Concentration row shows the concentration of the limiting reagent.
- When you type a concentration for a solvent, such as 0.1 M, Octet calculates the volume of that solvent.
- Reactants have Density and Volume rows. Octet knows the density of 41 common liquids, such as THF, DCM and triethylamine. Aiming to be expanded next update.
- When you type the Expected mass of a product, Octet calculates the amounts of the starting materials.
- When you type a Mass or a Volume for a reactant, Octet marks it as measured in red. To remove the mark, choose Clear measured mark.
- To add the temperature, the time and the atmosphere, click a cell and choose Conditions… in the Actions panel.
- A coefficient in a scheme, such as the 2 in 2 A + B → C, will set the equivalents. A fraction also works.
- The table calculates the molar mass of formulae with 27 more elements vs last week, such as Mo, Ba, Ce and Bi.
- When you type a mass with no unit, Octet uses grams. This can be configured Settings > Keyboard shortcuts.
- To copy the table as text for a spreadsheet, click a cell and choose Copy table in the Actions panel.
- To hide each row that has no value, choose Hide empty rows. To show them again, choose Show all rows and columns.
- To move between cells, press an arrow key or Tab. To edit a cell, press Return. To replace its value, type.
- To move a reagent table away from its scheme, drag it with the Select tool. The table stays there when you change the structures. It moves with its arrow.

## Experiment pages

My fave from new update

- The details at the top of an experiment page are normal text now so you can move, change or delete each name and each value.
- When you type the name of a detail, such as Pressure, you can click after it to type the value.
- To start each new experiment page with your own layout, choose Use for new documents in Settings > Document.
- The top portion is called the Experiment details.
- To set it up, click it with any tool except the Text tool.
- The text tool lets you change just one text box at a time.
- Each new experiment page has the next Exp. number. For example, after SE-A1-009 comes SE-A1-010. The home page’s Experiments list shows the number.
- When you type a formula on an experiment page, such as CO2, Na2SO4 or 1H NMR, Octet will sub- and super-script for you.
- When you start an experiment page, Octet adds a risk assessment on page 2. It lists each substance from the reagent table, with its formula and its CAS number. When Online search is on, Octet also adds the GHS pictograms and hazard codes.
- To remove one part of a risk assessment, such as a substance or a section, click it with Select and press Delete.
- To remove all of it, click its title and choose Remove risk assessment. To add it again, choose Add risk assessment.

## Drawing

- The periodic table, click and hold the text tool. You can then click any element, and use it as you’d expect.
- In the periodic table, hover over an element to see its atomic weight and its isotopes.
- Octet now draws each small fishhook arrow and each small curved arrow as one smooth curve.
- When you choose Structure > Clean Up Reaction, Octet turns each product to the orientation of its starting material.
- Augmented Clean Up Reaction is removed. Clean Up Reaction has incorporated the smart features and does the same work.
- When you change a structure, Octet also changes the values that you added from the Properties panel. To keep a value, you can type it manually in the caption.
- In Settings > Keyboard shortcuts, you can set a key for each circled charge, positive and negative.
- In a table, press Shift+Return to move up one cell.
- Fixes to action panel
- If you hover over at a collapsed section in the Actions panel, it opens but will not persist.
- To open a section permanently, click it. And vice versa
- You can click and drag a box over your files on home to Mass-remove

## Colour consistencies

- All the colour menus in Octet are now the same.
- It has recent colours, presets, your colours, Pick from screen and Add colour.
- To change the order of your colours, drag them. To rename or remove a colour, right-click it.
- To give part of a chemical label its own colour, select the characters. Then choose a colour.
- When you choose Colour > By Element a second time, Octet removes the element colours.

## 3D

- The Actions panel has a new section, 3D tools, with 3D clean up, depth shading and Flatten. They all work wonderfully I can confirm.
- When you choose 3D Clean Up, an allene, a ketene, an isocyanate and an azide stay straight as they should. ChemDraw struggles with this.
- When you choose 3D Clean Up on a flat drawing that you turned in 3D, the structure will become 3D. Try it with Cyclohexane
- When you choose 3D Clean Up or Flatten, or turn a structure in 3D, each stereocentre keeps its configuration
- To fade atoms in a 3D structure that has depth shading, select the atoms. Then move the Opacity slider in the Actions panel.

## Chemistry and search

- Convert Structure to Name can work with more ring systems, such as galantamine, brefeldin A, cefotaxime and the calixarenes.
- The Sequence tool draws peptides, RNA and DNA with a straight backbone. To straighten a chain that you drew, choose Structure > Clean Up Structure.
- When you paste HELM or FASTA text, Octet puts the sequence in the field of the Sequence tool. Press Return to draw it.
- To copy a peptide, RNA or DNA chain as text, choose Edit > Copy As > HELM or FASTA.
- To save each structure that a generic structure stands for, choose Structure > Expand Generic Structure to SDF.
- The Properties panel has a CAS number row. If Online search is on, Octet will search the numbers that it does not have locally from PubChem.
- When you type a CAS number in Name to Structure and PubChem finds it, Octet keeps the number. The next time you go to draw it, it won’t need to search so over time you’ll build a large offline repo!
- When you type [7]helicene, [7]circulene, [3]radialene, [6]cyclacene, [10]cyclophenacene or [2.2]metacyclophane, Name to Structure draws the structure.
- The Search menu has Search SciFinder, Search Google Scholar and Search Google Patents.
- You can get GHS hazard symbols from your structures pulled from PubChem, click Find hazards in the Properties panel. Online search must be on.

## Files

- In Settings > Document > Properties, you can set a title, the author and the keywords. Octet saves them in the file and in PDFs.
- To add a saved drawing or a picture to the open drawing, choose File > Insert…. You can also drop the file on the drawing.
- To use a ChemDraw style sheet (.cds), choose File > Apply Settings From File…. To save one, choose Settings > Document > Save to file…
- To show File > More Formats, set Settings > Pro > More formats to on. Pro is a new part of Settings.
- From File > More Formats, you can export CML, BMP and 3MF files. You can also open CIF and mmCIF files, and open and save .ct files.
- Export 3MF… makes a ball-and-stick model of your structures for a 3D printer.
- Export LaTeX (chemfig)… saves the structures as chemfig code in a .tex file.
- In a picture that you copy or export, each bond line and each arrow is a separate path. In Inkscape, you can move each one.
- To set the bond length, the line widths and the fonts of some objects only, select them. Then change the values in Settings > Pro > Selection.
- To print from the Export dialog, click Print

## Office / Apple suite / LibreOffice integration and Linux

- On Windows, a drawing that you paste into Word, PowerPoint or Excel exists as an Octet Drawing object. Double-click it to open it in Octet.
- To paste a picture only, set Settings > Import and export > Copy > Office object to off.
- On Mac, select a picture of a structure in Word and choose File > Edit Picture from Word. Octet opens the drawing.
- The first time, macOS asks if Octet Draw can control Microsoft Word. Click Allow.
- To open a structure in Word with a double-click on the Mac, turn on Settings > General > Word.
- On Linux, click Add to LibreOffice in Settings > General. Then double-click a structure in Writer, Impress or Draw to open it in Octet.
- On Windows and Linux, the Window menu lists the open drawings. A tick shows the drawing that is in front.
- On Linux, File > Print… and File > Print Selection… show the pages first, so you can see where the pages break.
- On Linux, each release build makes a Flatpak test version of Octet. It helps us find why drawing is slow on some NVIDIA GPUs. Thanks guys

## Fixes

- When you paste a drawing into Word on the Mac, the picture will remain sharp in Word for Windows. The classic supervisor - student workflow that has plagued us for so long!
- On Windows, a drawing that you paste into Word is its true size.
- 3D Clean Up keeps the configuration of stereocentres in six-membered rings. In 1.1.1, many got the opposite configuration.
- After 3D Clean Up, the SMILES, the InChI and the name of a structure have the correct configuration at each stereocentre.
- When you make many edits in a large drawing, Octet has much better crash resistance.
- A 3D structure with many identical groups, such as triisopropylsilyl groups, no longer stops fry octets brain for minutes.
- When a sulfuric or phosphoric amide has four or more identical groups, Convert Structure to Name uses tetrakis. In 1.1.1, the name contained "undefined".
- When you place a Newman projection template, the structure has its back carbon. In 1.1.1, the two carbons were broken and acted as one single C.
- When you set a text size such as 7.5 pt, Edit > Repeat applies that size again.
- When you press Escape in the Other size… field, the size does not change.
- When you rotate brackets with a structure, Octet counts the bonds that each bracket crosses in the turned position. (More smart basically)
- Octet reads negative charges correctly in big-endian CDX files, which old Mac versions of ChemDraw saved. (Small but someone asked haha)
- When you export a drawing as CDX and open it again in Octet, each compound number stays with its structure. Tables and catalytic cycles remain as Octet objects.
- When you open a CDX file that has a TLC plate, the spots have their correct size. In 1.1.1, the spots were too small to see which was annoying for some.
- If Octet cannot calculate a formula, the Actions panel shows a dash rather than crashing.
- If Octet cannot load its chemistry engine, a notice tells you to restart Octet.
