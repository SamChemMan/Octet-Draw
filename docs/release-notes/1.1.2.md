# Octet Draw 1.1.2

This update fixes problems you reported and adds features you asked for.

## Highlights

- Minimise a spectrum, and it becomes a small pill beside its structure.
- Octet predicts ¹⁹F and ³¹P NMR spectra.
- The template library has 14 new sets and 211 new templates, such as lab equipment, cells, organisms and DNA.
- Every colour menu is the same, and Pick from screen takes a colour from anywhere.
- The stoichiometry table reads reagents over the arrow, ions and coefficients.
- On Windows, a drawing pasted into Word keeps its true size.

## Spectra

- Minimise a spectrum, and it becomes a small pill beside its structure. Click the pill to show the spectrum again, as you left it.
- The spectrum and the Actions panel take turns. When one opens, the other becomes its pill.
- Every panel has the same controls at the top right: Minimise, Full screen and Close.
- Undo shows a spectrum again after you close it.
- Mass spec analysis is in the Actions panel and the Structure menu, next to the NMR predictions.
- View > Show or Hide Spectrum minimises the spectrum or shows it again.
- Each structure has its own spectra, as tabs. Click + to add a spectrum. Close a tab to remove it.
- Drag the top edge of the spectrum to make it taller or shorter.
- Use the arrow keys to move between the peaks. A screen reader reads each peak.

## NMR

- Octet predicts ¹⁹F and ³¹P NMR spectra.
- ¹H and ¹³C spectra show the splitting from fluorine and phosphorus.
- A ¹⁹F spectrum has its ¹H couplings by default. A ³¹P spectrum has none by default. Decouple ¹H changes this.
- Octet opens measured ¹⁹F and ³¹P spectra. When a file does not name its nucleus, Octet finds it from the frequency.
- You can predict ¹⁹F and ³¹P spectra for every structure. A structure with no fluorine or phosphorus shows an empty spectrum.
- Octet predicts ¹H and ¹³C shifts more accurately near sulfonyl groups, in heteroaromatic rings and in bridged bicyclic rings.
- Octet marks OH, NH and SH shifts as Rough, because the solvent changes them.

## Templates

- The Lab set has 29 more items, such as a three-neck flask, a Dean-Stark trap, a Büchner funnel and a balloon.
- The new Lab equipment set has 22 items, such as a microscope, a micropipette, a well plate and a gel.
- The new Cells set has cells, organelles, bacteria and viruses.
- The new Biomolecules and BioArt sets have membranes, receptors, an antibody, a DNA double helix, tRNA, a plasmid and more.
- The new Anatomy, Organisms and Bugs sets have organs, lab model organisms such as the mouse and the zebrafish, and insects.
- The new Symbols set has stars, polygons, a tick mark, warning signs, the radiation symbol and GHS hazard diamonds.
- The new DNA and RNA set has the nucleosides, the nucleotides, ATP and the A·T, G·C and A·U base pairs.
- The new Side chains and Peptides sets have the 20 amino acid side chains and peptide backbones of 2 to 5 residues.
- The new Polyhedra set has the coordination geometries, from linear to pentagonal bipyramidal, and cages with their hidden edges dashed.
- The new Supramolecules and Carbon sets have crown ethers, cryptands, calixarenes, C60, nanotubes and a graphene flake.
- The new sets load after Octet starts, so it starts as fast as before.

## Drawing

- Every colour menu has the same parts in the same order: recent colours, presets, your colours, Pick from screen and Add colour.
- Pick from screen takes a colour from the drawing or from anywhere on the screen.
- Drag your colours into a new order, or right-click one to rename or remove it.
- You can give part of a chemical label its own colour. Select the characters in the label, then pick a colour.
- To remove one hydrogen from an atom, put the pointer on it and press its element key again, as in ChemDraw. Press O twice on OH to get a bare O.
- One bond on its own, such as ethane, is now a molecule. So is one labelled atom, such as H₂O.
- The Text tool has a periodic table. Hold the Text tool and choose an element. Point at an element to see its atomic weight and isotopes.
- You can fade selected atoms in a 3D structure with depth shading. Use the Opacity slider in the Actions panel.
- Small fishhook and curved arrows draw as one smooth curve. Their arrowheads bend with the arc, also in exports.
- Values that you add from the Properties panel update when you change the structure. To stop the update, type new text in the caption.
- You can set a key for circled positive and negative charges in Settings > Keyboard shortcuts. They have no key by default.
- Add multi-centre attachment and Add variable attachment have new, clear icons. In the Actions panel the two buttons stay side by side.
- In the Actions panel, one click closes a section that opened on hover. Its arrow points down while the section is open.
- Use File > Insert… to add a saved drawing or a picture to the open drawing. You can also drop the file onto the drawing.
- Press Return on a focused button in a window, such as Cancel, and Octet uses that button.
- On Home, drag a box over drawings to select them. Click Remove to take the selected drawings off Home or out of the folder.

## Chemistry

- The stoichiometry table reads more reagents above and below the arrow, such as THF, cat. H2SO4 and K2CO3 (2 eq.).
- It also reads ions and words, such as H+, OH−, proton and water.
- A coefficient in the scheme, such as the 2 in 2 A + B → C, sets the equivalents.
- A mass typed without a unit is now in grams. To use milligrams, set Settings > Typing > Mass without a unit.
- Reactants always show Density and Volume rows. Type a product's Expected mass, and Octet calculates the amounts of the starting materials.
- Convert Structure to Name names more ring systems, for example galantamine, brefeldin A, cefotaxime and the calixarenes.
- The Sequence tool draws peptides, RNA and DNA with a straight backbone. Clean Up does the same for a chain you draw.
- Paste HELM or FASTA to open it as a sequence. Edit > Copy As has HELM and FASTA.
- Structure > Expand Generic Structure to SDF… saves every structure in a series to an SD file, up to 10,000.
- The Properties panel shows the CAS number of each structure. With Online search on, Octet asks PubChem for numbers it does not have.

## Files

- Set the title, author and keywords of a drawing in Settings > Document > Properties. Octet saves them in the file and in exported PDFs.
- A new drawing gets your computer user name as its author.
- File > Apply Settings From File… opens ChemDraw style sheets (.cds). Settings > Document > Save to file… saves your style as a .cds file.
- Settings has a new Pro tab. Its More formats switch adds File > More Formats, for rare file formats.
- File > More Formats exports CML, BMP and 3MF, imports and exports connection tables (.ct), and copies SLN.
- Export 3MF… makes a ball-and-stick model of your structures for 3D printing.
- Export LaTeX (chemfig) saves structures as chemfig code for LaTeX. Rings, wedges, charges and labels match the page.
- Open crystal structures (CIF) and the ligands in mmCIF files from File > More Formats. Octet keeps their 3D shape as depth.
- Settings > Pro > Selection sets the bond length, line widths and fonts of the selected objects. Copy settings and Paste settings give them to another object.

## Search

- The Search menu has Search SciFinder, Search Google Scholar and Search Google Patents.
- Search SciFinder opens SciFinder and copies the structure's SMILES, so you can paste it there.
- With Online search on, Find hazards in the Properties panel shows GHS pictograms and hazard and precautionary statements from PubChem.

## Windows, Linux and Word

- On Windows, a drawing pasted into Word keeps its true size. Before, it grew 7.5 times when its size was set back to 100%.
- On Windows, Word can start Octet to open a drawing object. Octet now waits for the drawing and does not close too early.
- On Windows and Linux, the Window menu lists the open drawings, with a tick on the drawing in front.
- On Linux, Print and Print Selection show the pages first, so you can see where the pages break.
- On Linux, a Flatpak build is available for computers with NVIDIA graphics, where drawing can be slow.

## Fixes

- A large drawing no longer stops the editor after many edits. If Octet cannot work out a formula, the Actions panel shows a dash.
- If Octet's chemistry engine cannot load, Octet tells you.
