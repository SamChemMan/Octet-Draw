# Octet Draw 1.1.2

This update adds features you asked for.

## Highlights

- The template library has 14 new sets and 211 new templates, such as lab equipment, cells, organisms and DNA.

## Spectra

- Minimise a spectrum, and it becomes a small pill beside its structure. Click the pill to show the spectrum again, as you left it.
- The spectrum and the Actions panel take turns. When one opens, the other becomes its pill.
- Every panel has the same controls at the top right: Minimise, Full screen and Close.
- Undo shows a spectrum again after you close it.
- Mass spec analysis is in the Actions panel and the Structure menu, next to the NMR predictions.
- View > Show or Hide Spectrum minimises the spectrum or shows it again.
- Each structure has its own spectra, as tabs. Click + to add a spectrum. Close a tab to remove it.
- Drag the top edge of the spectrum to make it taller or shorter.
- Use the arrow keys to move between the peaks. A screen reader reads each peak.

## NMR

- Octet predicts ¹⁹F and ³¹P NMR spectra.
- ¹H and ¹³C spectra show the splitting from fluorine and phosphorus.
- A ¹⁹F spectrum has its ¹H couplings by default. A ³¹P spectrum has none by default. Decouple ¹H changes this.
- Octet opens measured ¹⁹F and ³¹P spectra. When a file does not name its nucleus, Octet finds it from the frequency.

## Templates

- The Lab set has 29 more items, such as a three-neck flask, a Dean-Stark trap, a Büchner funnel and a balloon.
- The new Lab equipment set has 22 items, such as a microscope, a micropipette, a well plate and a gel.
- The new Cells set has cells, organelles, bacteria and viruses.
- The new Biomolecules and BioArt sets have membranes, receptors, an antibody, a DNA double helix, tRNA, a plasmid and more.
- The new Anatomy, Organisms and Bugs sets have organs, lab model organisms such as the mouse and the zebrafish, and insects.
- The new Symbols set has stars, polygons, a tick mark, warning signs, the radiation symbol and GHS hazard diamonds.
- The new DNA and RNA set has the nucleosides, the nucleotides, ATP and the A·T, G·C and A·U base pairs.
- The new Side chains and Peptides sets have the 20 amino acid side chains and peptide backbones of 2 to 5 residues.
- The new Polyhedra set has the coordination geometries, from linear to pentagonal bipyramidal, and cages with their hidden edges dashed.
- The new Supramolecules and Carbon sets have crown ethers, cryptands, calixarenes, C60, nanotubes and a graphene flake.
- The new sets load after Octet starts, so it starts as fast as before.
