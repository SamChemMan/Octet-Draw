# Stereo projection tools (PR #150)

Prof. Schramm's PR #150 ("Stereo projection tools: Invert Configuration Here, Fischer ⇄ wedge, D/L, steroid α/β, Ξ"),
ported onto release-1.1.3 at 9b215103 on `item/c-ss150`. Sam approved porting all his PRs. His code was adapted from
ReShiki (MIT OR Apache-2.0); the credit is in each file and in `THIRD_PARTY.md`.

## What the PR does

1. Structure > Invert Configuration Here: one selected stereocentre changes R ⇄ S, one Undo step. Wedge drawings swap
   their wedges and hashes; Fischer, Haworth, chair and 3D drawings move the groups (a half turn, so groups inside them
   keep their own configuration). Refused, with a reason, unless only that centre changed.
2. Structure > Convert to Fischer Projection: one acyclic structure, the longest carbon chain vertical, the more
   oxidised end at the top, an explicit H on each centre. Refused unless the InChI is the same.
3. Structure > Convert to Wedge Drawing: a structure drawn in depth (Fischer, Haworth, chair, 3D) drawn flat with
   wedges. Refused unless the InChI is the same.
4. A Fischer projection turned by more than 10° is no longer read as one. The configuration stays (the depth holds it).
5. Settings > Drawing > Stereochemistry, all off: D and L names ("L (S)"), steroid α and β (3β, 5α) in place of R and
   S, and a mark on undefined centres, (?) or (Ξ), with (ξ) for a pseudoasymmetric one.
6. CDXML: these names go as plain text, with ShowAtomStereo no on those atoms so ChemDraw adds no R or S of its own.
7. The PR also carries #149 (template attachment: `attach.ts`, `TemplateLibrary.tsx`, favourites, the green
   preview), because it was cut on top of it.

## What Octet already has

- #145 on the release: `redrawStereo` and `placeWedges` in `chem.ts`, `keepHands` (Flatten keeps each hand), the
  stereo warnings, `stereo.check.ts`. Redraw Stereo Bonds leaves 3D and perspective drawings alone, so it does not do
  Convert to Wedge Drawing's job.
- Fischer, Haworth and chair templates, all drawn with depth (`library.json`).
- Show Stereochemistry with R, S, E and Z, and Parentheses round R and S.
- #149 is ported already as Octet's own design (741be539). Nothing of it comes again from this PR.

ChemDraw (notes in `~/octet-research/notes`, help 26 and the 22.2 tutorials): no Fischer reading (a Fischer cross in
ChemDraw sets no stereochemistry), no invert-one-centre command, no D/L, α/β or Ξ labels. ChemDraw's Flip inverts every
centre at once. So all of this goes past ChemDraw. Nothing to match.

## What is ported

- `src/projection.ts`: Invert Configuration Here, Fischer recognition, Convert to Fischer Projection, Convert to Wedge
  Drawing. His logic, with the messages in STE and a "drawing is not changed" end on each refusal. Two small helpers
  (`invertible`, `fischerable`) decide when the Actions panel offers the commands.
- `src/stereonames.ts`: D and L, steroid α and β, undefined marks. His logic. Octet adds a cache by structure (chem.ts
  `keyed`, the key Show Stereochemistry's R and S already use), so a drag does not work the names out again.
- `cip.ts handIn`: a hand in any neighbour order, three neighbours with the H last. `chem.ts wedgesFrom`: wedges from
  the hands a depth drawing gives, placed by `placeWedges`.
- `cdxml.ts`: the names as text, ShowAtomStereo no. Octet adds `cip` to the atom's own data, so a CDXML file Octet
  reads back keeps Show Stereochemistry on for those atoms (the PR lost it).
- Wiring, as Redraw Stereo Bonds has it: the Structure menu (`main.rs`, so its keys can be set in Settings > Keyboard
  shortcuts), the command palette with search words, the Actions panel (3D tools set: Invert on an atom, Convert to
  Wedge Drawing on a structure with depth, Convert to Fischer Projection on one acyclic structure), MCP command ids
  (behind AI = false; the server does not start in 1.1.3).
- Settings: the three rows under Stereochemistry; labels "D and L names", "Steroid α and β", "Undefined stereocentres"
  (None, (?), (Ξ)).

## What is left out, and why

- All of #149 (attach.ts, the template library changes, favourites, the green preview, `stampTemplate`): Octet has its
  own port.
- The "Configuration inverted. Undo restores the original." notice: Octet says a done command to screen readers
  (`sayAfter`), as Redraw Stereo Bonds does, and shows no notice when it works. Show, not tell.
- Open-chain ketoses get no D or L, and Convert to Fischer Projection takes acyclic structures only (as the PR).

## Stress list

- Every centre of the 14 sugar and amino acid templates (Haworth, chair, Fischer): invert, only that centre changes,
  one InChI stereo entry changes, inverting twice gives the original.
- α-glucose C-1 → β-glucose; β-glucose C-2 → β-mannose; D-glucose (Fischer) C-5 → L-idose.
- A wedge, a hollow wedge, a ChemDraw kept configuration with no stereo bond; a Fischer arm with its own centre; an arm
  that joins the chain again (refused); atoms that are no centre (refused).
- Fischer from wedges and back: D-glucose, L-glucose, D-threose, L-serine, L-threonine, D-erythrose, L-alanine, with
  the D or L kept each way; the D-glucose template to wedges and back to four Fischer centres.
- Refused: a ring, a centre off the chain, an E double bond, no centre, two structures, a flat drawing to wedges.
- Turns of 9°, 11°, 90°, 180° and 270°: the configuration stays; Fischer reading only within 10° of the axes.
- D and L on the 19 chiral amino acids and their D forms (L-cysteine is L (R)), the 8 aldohexoses both ways, the
  Haworth, chair and Fischer sugars, xylopyranose, arabinopyranose, a dipeptide; none on glycine or an α-amino ketone.
- Steroid faces on two androstanediols, cholesterol (C-20 keeps R or S), estradiol, the mirror image, norethisterone's
  C-17.
- (?), (Ξ), (ξ) on 2,3,4-trihydroxyglutaric acid's C-3; none on a set centre or a wavy bond.
- CDXML: names as text with the R italic, ShowAtomStereo no, Octet skips its own text and keeps Show Stereochemistry on
  reading back; a Fischer projection and an inverted Haworth sugar through CDXML with no Octet data keep the compound
  and the D or L; `.oct` keeps it all.
- Speed: Show Stereochemistry with the names off does no new work (the R and S loop only). With all three on,
  cholesterol costs 0.7 ms the first time and 0.14 ms after (kept by structure); a structure moved whole is not worked
  out again.
