# Issue 59 (reopened): what PR 127 had and the chemfig writer leaves out

Branch `item/b-59`, from `release-1.1.3` f06d1b49, 19 November 2026. For release 1.1.3 (Friday 20 November). Sam
approved the build. The writer is `src/chemfig.ts` (decision 0028); the earlier plan is
`docs/plans/issue-59-chemfig-copy.md`.

konokonekonoko reopened the issue on 15 November: PR 127 had features the merged writer does not have. PR 127 is
Apache-2.0 code contributed to this repository. Its logic is reused here, written into the current writer's
structure. The commit and the code header credit konokonekonoko. The PR is left as it is.

## What it must do

Every ask, from the task and PR 127's "Implemented" list that the writer leaves out:

1. **Reaction schemes.** A reaction that Octet reads (`reaction.ts` `findReaction`, `interpret.ts`) is written as
   `\schemestart … \schemestop`: its compounds in the row's order, `\+` for each plus sign, and an `\arrow` for each
   arrow with its kind and its conditions text above and below it.
2. **Curved and electron-pushing arrows** with `\chemmove`: curved, fishhook and double-headed arcs, lines, arcs, and
   a reaction or resonance arrow that is not in a scheme.
3. **Polymer brackets** with `\polymerdelim`: brackets, parentheses and braces round atoms, one side or both, turned,
   with the usage label at the lower right (n, co, mix, a count) and the mark and repeat tags at the upper right.
4. **Names and compound numbers** under structures with `\chemname*`.
5. **Atom numbers and map numbers** where the page shows them.
6. **R/S and E/Z labels** where Show Stereochemistry shows them, with or without the parentheses
   (Settings > Drawing > Parentheses round R and S).
7. Compact and readable modes stay. The readable lines, trimmed and joined, are the compact code (a scheme's lines
   end with `%`, which the join takes off).
8. The notice lists only what the code still does not have.
9. Checks: `src/chemfig.check.ts` and `src/chemfigcopy-stress.check.ts` get a case for each.

## How

All in `src/chemfig.ts`. The writer keeps its walk, its ring syntax and its passes. New options:
`schemes` (the reactions, by atoms and ids) and `stereo` (`chem.ts` `stereoLabels` and the parentheses setting).
`src/Editor.tsx` `chemfigOf` gives both, for the drawing or the selection (other parts, such as an AI app's ids, get
neither; their structures are written as before and the notice names the stereo labels).

- **Blocks.** Structures are split into blocks only when the drawing has a scheme or a name: each structure joined
  with the others its names or its scheme item cover (a salt's ions) is a `\chemfig` of its own. With neither, the
  code is one `\chemfig` as before, byte for byte. Each pass writes every block in the order they come out, so the
  trace and the hooks stay as they are.
- **Schemes** (PR 127's `arrowCode`): `\arrow{type[above][below]}[angle,coeff]`, at the arrow's angle and length (a
  bond is 3em, an arrow 5em at coeff 1). Types: `->`, `<=>` (equilibrium), `<->` (resonance), `-/>` (no-go, both
  kinds). Retrosynthetic, dipole and block arrows are written `->` and the notice says so. A structure on an arrow is
  a compact `\chemfig`; text is in text mode, more than one line in `\shortstack`. Readable code puts each piece on a
  line ended by `%`. An arrow that points left has its above and below swapped, as chemfig puts the first label on
  the arrow's left.
- **`\chemmove`** (PR 127's `moves`, `arcHalf`): each end, and a curve's middle, is a node `@{name}` hung off the
  nearest atom (not a fusion atom) by a bond chemfig does not draw. An arc is two TikZ `to[bend …,looseness …]`
  halves through its middle. Heads are chemfig's `CF` tip, half heads its harpoon; colour, width, dashed, double and
  wavy as on the page. Node names carry a hash of the drawing, so two drawings in one document do not clash.
- **`\polymerdelim`** (PR 127's `brackets`): the bracket's two side middles are nodes hung off its block's first
  atom, with empty atoms at its corners so the picture's box holds it. Height and depth from the bracket, turned with
  `rotate` and `h align=false`, one side by `delimiters={[.}`.
- **`\chemname*{\chemfig{…}}{name}`**: the captions under a block's atoms, in id order, lines joined by `\\`. Text runs
  keep bold, italic, underline, scripts and colour (`\textbf`, `\textit`, `\underline`, `\textsuperscript`,
  `\textsubscript`, `\textcolor[RGB]`). Characters as for labels; others are left out and named.
- **Atom numbers, map numbers, R/S**: more `\charge` items on the atom. An atom number and an R/S go in the gaps
  between the bonds as the page puts them (Editor `besideAtom`'s rule: widest gap, away from the ring). A map number
  goes where `render()` draws it (`maps`); level with a label of two atoms it goes on that label's end atom.
- **E/Z**: an atom of its own, `{\scriptstyle(\mathit{E})}`, hung off an end of the bond (not a fusion atom) at the
  page's spot. The spot is `stereoSpot` in `render.ts`, now shared with the Editor's Show Stereochemistry marks.
- **Comment lines**: the wavy line's, and one for `\polymerdelim` and `\chemmove`, which need two LaTeX runs.

Hot paths: `render.ts` gains one exported function (`stereoSpot`) that the Editor called inline. No drawing, dragging,
selection or file-reading code changes. The writer runs only on Copy, Export and `read`.

## ChemDraw cross-check (notes only)

ChemDraw has no chemfig or LaTeX output (`~/octet-research/notes/chemdraw26-inventory.md` item 2: Export is PDF,
PNG, SVG, TIFF, EPS, CDXML, CDX, MOL, SDF, RXN; `menus/live-menus.md`: Copy As has no TeX). So the check is on the
objects the code carries:

- Show Stereochemistry: R/S, E/Z and, since 25.0, M/P on allenes and atropisomers (inventory item 5, line 174).
  Octet's `stereoLabels` gives M/P on the atom where it shows them, so they are written as R/S are.
- Atom numbers (Show Atom Number) and reaction map numbers (inventory line 129 and the map number notes in
  `render.ts`, which follow ChemDraw's placing).
- Brackets (inventory 675–684): brackets, parentheses, braces; usages with their labels (n, co, alt, blk, ran, xl,
  grf, mer, f, mix, mod, mon, #, c, anyp); repeat pattern (hh, eu) and flip; Octet's one side and its ‡, †, +, −
  marks. Each shows in `\polymerdelim` as the page draws it.
- Arrows (inventory 839–851): straight (reaction, equilibrium, resonance, retrosynthetic, no-go cross and hash,
  dipole, block), curved at each sweep, fishhook, double-headed. chemfig has no retrosynthetic, hashed, dipole or
  block arrow type in a scheme, so they are written as the nearest and named in the notice.

## Stress list

Each is a check in `src/chemfig.check.ts` or `src/chemfigcopy-stress.check.ts` (ST21 to ST30):

1. A two-step scheme A + B → C → D with text above and a structure below one arrow; readable and compact.
2. Each straight arrow kind in a scheme; a leftward arrow swaps its labels; a long arrow gets its coeff.
3. Conditions of two lines (`\shortstack`), hostile text (`%`, `#`, `\input`, `}`) in a name and a condition.
4. A curved arrow from a lone pair to a bond, a fishhook, a double-headed arc, a line, and an equilibrium arrow
   outside a scheme (still named).
5. Brackets: both sides, one side, turned, an SRU with n, a mark, a bracket round nothing (named).
6. Names: one structure with a name and a number, two named structures (two `\chemfig`), a salt with one name.
7. Atom numbers on a chain and a ring; map numbers on a label of two atoms (level) and a skeletal carbon.
8. R/S with and without parentheses, E/Z on a chain double bond and on a ring's exocyclic bond, a coloured E/Z.
9. Every block of every new case is read strictly and replayed (atoms within 0.02 bond lengths) and read at TeX's
   level (`texLint`, which now reads each `\chemfig`, the text outside them, and the comment lines).
10. 300 salts with names and a 100-step scheme: within the time budget, no deep nesting.

## Cut, and why

- **No TeX here.** Nothing is compiled; the strict reader and `texLint` stand in. PR 127's author compiled these
  commands with pdfLaTeX (MiKTeX). The arrow-label swap for leftward arrows and the `\polymerdelim` keys are from
  chemfig's manual as read, not from a compile.
- **Branching schemes and catalytic cycles**: chemfig's schemes are one row here; a wrapped row is one long row
  (PR 127 cut them too).
- **Captions read as chemistry** (Interpret Chemically) are written as text in a scheme, not as `\chemfig` labels.
- **Equilibrium, retrosynthetic, no-go, dipole and block arrows outside a scheme, orbitals, fragmentation lines and
  pen curves** are not drawn; the notice names them (PR 127 cut them too).
- **An AI app's `read` and the headless host** get no schemes or stereo labels (they have no reaction reading of a
  part by ids). 1.1.3 ships with AI off.
- **Free text, shapes, tables, pictures, spectra, highlights, ring fills, depth shading, query properties, turned
  labels**: not in chemfig; still named.
