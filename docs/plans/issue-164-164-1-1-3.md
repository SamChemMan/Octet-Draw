# An atom's charge with its own place, colour and size (#164, 1.1.3)

Issue #164 (swaldvoge-eth, with spirokai). Stand-in labels (Nu, E, LG) and free charges shipped in
1.1.2. What is still missing is spirokai's ask: a charge that belongs to an atom can only swing round the atom at a
fixed distance (`edit.ts` moveMark sets `Atom.angles.charge`). It cannot be selected, coloured or sized on its own.

Branch `item/b-164`, cut from `release-1.1.3` at f06d1b49, for 1.1.3 (Friday 20 November). Sam approved the build.

## What it must do

1. A click on an atom's charge with Select picks the charge alone (not its atom). The pick shows a selection frame
   round the charge. A click anywhere else, or picking anything else, drops it.
2. A drag on the charge moves it freely: any direction and any distance up to 3 bond lengths from the atom (at the
   atom's scale). Fixed Angles on keeps the direction in 15° steps (Option frees it), as the old drag did. After the
   drag the charge is picked.
3. The picked charge takes a colour from the usual colour controls (the Actions panel's colour menu
   and the colour picker). Black clears it back to the atom's colour.
4. The picked charge takes a size from the size control a free symbol has (the Actions panel's size, in pt). The
   size is a factor of the atom's sign size (0.75 of its label size), kept between 0.2 and 5.
5. It stays the atom's charge: the formula, mass, name, SMILES, checks and valence count it as before.
6. Every charge kind: plain + and − on a skeletal carbon and on a label, 2+ and higher, circled ⊕ and ⊖ with or
   without a number, and a radical ion's dot and sign as one unit (the dot takes the charge's colour and size).
7. The picked charge has the charge actions of a free charge: Add a positive charge, Add a negative charge, Circle
   the charge, and Delete (which takes the charge off the atom). The Actions panel names it and shows its kind
   buttons: the four charge kinds (a radical ion: its two kinds).
8. The place, colour and size move, turn, flip and scale with the atom (the place is an offset from the atom in
   bond lengths at the atom's scale).
9. Files: .oct (its JSON and its CDXML), CDXML and CDX write the charge as ChemDraw keeps a charge it was given its
   own place: a Symbol graphic with `represent attribute="Charge"` linked to the node, at its own place, with its
   own `color`, and its size as the length of its BoundingBox. Octet's own data on the node keeps the exact values.
   Old files read as before.
10. Rendering, PNG, SVG, PDF, EMF, Copy and Word pictures all come from the renderer, so they follow.

## How

Model (`render.ts` Atom): `sign?: { at?: [x, y]; colour?: string; size?: number }`. at: the middle of the charge's
ink (a radical ion: of its dot and sign), from the atom, in bond lengths at the atom's scale, y up. When set it wins
over `angles.charge`. size: the factor. colour: #rrggbb. `readSign` checks every value (finite, distance up to 3,
size 0.2 to 5, colour a hex) and is used by every reader.

Renderer (`render.ts`):
- A sign with its own place is drawn as a skeletal carbon's sign is: a mark of its own (not part of the label's
  outline, so bonds are not cut by it and the label's box does not grow), in the label's face.
- Size: the sign's text, a radical ion's dot and gap, and a circled charge's ring and number scale by the factor.
- Colour: the sign's text, the ion dot and the ring are drawn in the charge's colour (a new per-atom list of the
  charge's polygons beside the atom's marks).
- `Drawing.charges`: each charge's ink box (px), for the pick frame and the drag.
- A turned label: a sign with its own place stays where it was put.

Edit (`edit.ts`): `moveSign` (free drag with limit and 15° snap), `setSign` (colour, size), `signKind` (the kind
buttons), and `turnMarks` turns and flips `sign.at` too.

Editor: `signPick` (the atom whose charge is picked), set by a click or drag on the charge with Select, cleared by
any other press or pick. The Actions panel gets a `shape` context with `attached` for it. Commands routed to it:
`colour.*`, `caption.size.*`, `shape.symbol.*`, `charge.*`, `delete`.

CDXML (`cdxml.ts`): writing, a label's sign with its own place leaves the label text (it is a Symbol linked to the
node, as a sign moved off a label already is); a skeletal or circled charge's Symbol goes to its own place; the
Symbol's `color` is the charge's colour and its BoundingBox length its size. A sign of 2 or more on a label stays in
the label text (a Symbol holds one charge, as now) in its colour. Octet's data on the node gets `sign`. Reading,
Octet's own files take `sign` from that data. A ChemDraw file's linked charge Symbol gives the colour when it differs
from the node's, the size when it differs from the atom's sign size by more than 5%, and the place when it is more
than 0.8 bond lengths from the atom. Nearer than that it keeps only its angle round the atom, as before: ChemDraw's
own default places in Sam's corpus are 0.42 to 0.66 bond lengths out (17 linked charge symbols in 11 files), so
files that were never moved by hand read exactly as they did.

CDX: the CDX writer and reader go through the same CDXML tree, so CDX follows. .oct: file.ts writes and reads `sign`.

## ChemDraw cross-check (from notes only)

- ChemDraw help (Chemical Annotations, in `~/octet-research/notes/help26`): an attached chemical symbol can be moved
  any distance from its related atom; unattached symbols can be resized; radical cation and radical anion symbols
  rotate and resize from their charge end.
- `gaps.md` symbols-and-represent: a Symbol is a graphic with SymbolType, linked by `represent` (object and
  attribute), and the node keeps `Charge`.
- `chemdraw-inventory.md` Chemical symbol: ChemDraw's symbol takes the generic object menu (Front/Back, Flip,
  Rotate, Scale) and the Color menu; Octet had "no separate object to move".
- Sam's corpus: linked Plus, Minus, CirclePlus, CircleMinus, RadicalCation and RadicalAnion symbols at 0.42 to 0.66
  bond lengths, size 7.5 (a radical ion 3.75 per half), no own colours.

Octet matches: own place (any distance, with Octet's limit of 3 bond lengths), own colour, own size, linked by
represent in CDXML and CDX. Octet differs: a radical ion does not rotate (ChemDraw's does); a picked charge is not
turned, flipped, ordered or grouped on its own (it follows its atom).

## Stress cases

1. Skeletal C+ moved to (1, 1): drawn there, formula unchanged, CDXML Symbol at that place, round trip exact.
2. N+ on a label moved far: sign not in the label text; label box unchanged; bonds not cut by it.
3. N2+ on a label with own colour: text sign in its colour; round trip keeps the colour.
4. Circled ⊖ with a number 2, own size 2: ring and number twice the size.
5. Radical cation unit moved and coloured: dot and sign in one colour, written as RadicalCation.
6. Offset beyond 3 bond lengths in a file: refused (left out), not clamped from hostile data.
7. Size 0, NaN, 1e9, a colour "red" or "#12": left out.
8. Atom turned 90° and flipped: offset turns and flips with it.
9. Charge removed (0): no Symbol written, no sign data written.
10. A ChemDraw file with a symbol at 0.5 bond lengths: reads as before (angle only); at 1.5: keeps the place.
11. ChemDraw colour on a symbol equal to the node's: no own colour.
12. CDX round trip of 1 to 5.
13. Old .oct and CDXML from 1.1.2: read the same (no sign).
14. Hot path: render timing of a drawing with many charges no slower.

## Cut

- Turning a radical ion symbol by its own handle (ChemDraw's rotation handle on the symbol): it needs a turn on the
  attached charge and on its CDXML ends. Left for later.
- Resize handles on the picked charge: it sizes by the size control and the keys a free symbol takes; the selection
  frame has no handles.
- Arrow-key nudging of the picked charge alone.
- The MCP: 1.1.3 ships with AI off, and the MCP already places a charge by `charge_angle`; its own place, colour and
  size wait for the MCP session.
