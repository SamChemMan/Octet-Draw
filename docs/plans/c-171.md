# Issue 171: the top of a picture is cut off in Word for Mac

Branch `item/c-171`, from release-1.1.3 (9ca16e2). For 1.1.3.

## What we found

The reporter's two screenshots (Word for Mac, 1.1.2) show cuts at the top and at the right edge only, never at the
left or bottom. Measured against Arial's glyph boxes (B is 5.405 pt wide and 7.158 pt tall at 10 pt):

| screenshot | top | right |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (ring corner) | 0.88 pt off the ring's top corner | 0.47 pt off the r of Br |
| 2 (Br) | 0.61 pt off the top of Br | 0.47 pt off the r of Br |

Octet's own picture is not cut. Bromobenzene and benzene copied in WebKit the way Copy does it (pictureOf, then the
Mac's clipboard PDF from mac_picture) hold all their ink: usvg's ink box is within 0.005 pt of each edge, and Quartz
draws the PDF at 2400 dpi with the ink touching each edge and nothing lost.

So Word cuts it. The cut is less than 1 pt, it is at the top and right only, and it differs with the picture's height
but not its width (the two schemes have the same width). That fits the fraction of a point in the picture's size:
Word for Mac seems to draw a PDF picture in whole points, from its bottom-left corner, as PDF counts (not seen in
Word itself: no Word run was made for this). Word's own copies of a PDF
are whole points (a 65.9 pt PDF became a 66 pt picture, `~/octet-research/clipboard/word-octet-pdf`). ChemDraw on the
Mac rounds its clipboard PDF's MediaBox out to whole points (`notes/acs-render/rules.md`, export-frame: 86 by 65 pt
for 85.44 by 64.30 pt of ink), and Word does not cut ChemDraw's pictures.

A margin of 0.5 pt (the first plan) does not change the fraction, so screenshot 1 would still lose 0.38 pt of the
ring. The size has to be whole points.

## What it must do

1. Copy's picture is whole points wide and high, with the drawing in the middle: the size of the ink rounded up to
   the next whole point, the spare split equally between the two sides (0 to 0.5 pt a side). Every system: the Mac's
   PDF and Office copy, the Windows EMF, the Linux PNG. Test: export.check.ts, `onWholePoints` on an SVG of
   56.21 by 36.55 pt gives 57 by 37 pt and its view box moves by half the spare; a whole size stays as it is.
2. Update in Word (Mac, word.rs) and the Office object's picture (Windows, ole_windows.rs) get the same picture.
   Test: export.check.ts reads Editor.tsx: Copy and every `ole_update` call take `copyPicture`.
3. Export is as it was: its own margin setting, from the tight frame. Test: the existing export.check.ts asserts that
   Export takes `pictureOf`; plus one that `copyPicture` is not in Export's code.
4. Rust: for L-proline as 1.1.3 copies it, the PDF's MediaBox and the EMF frame are whole points and the ink is
   within 0.5 pt of each edge, the same on both sides. Test: vector.rs `the_frame_fits_the_ink`.

## Design

`onWholePoints(svg)` in src/export.ts, beside `withMargin`, which it calls with a margin for each axis. Editor.tsx gets
`copyPicture(part)`: `pictureOf` then `onWholePoints`. Copy (NATIVE_CLIP branch) and the three `ole_update` calls use
it. No Rust code changes. The EMF+ records and the clipboard's order of types are as they were.

Speed: one regular expression on the picture's first tag, once a copy. Not a hot path.
