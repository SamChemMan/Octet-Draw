## Register

product

## Users

- Undergraduate and A-level students who struggle with ChemDraw or have no licence after they graduate.
- PhD students and postdocs who draw schemes, mechanisms, catalytic cycles and SI figures every day. They want speed, layout tools, CDXML round-trips and ACS defaults.
- Teachers who make worksheets and slides and often have no licence.
- Chemists at small biotechs with no site licence.
- Former ChemSketch and Marvin users who just lost a free tool.

They work at a desk, mostly on a laptop, often with a paper or slide deadline. The primary task on screen is always the drawing.

## Product Purpose

Octet is a free, native, offline desktop app for drawing chemical structures, reactions and schemes. It replaces ChemDraw. Success: a student draws aspirin in under a minute with no help, an expert draws a four-step catalytic cycle in under three minutes, and ChemDraw files open, change and save with no visible loss.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quiet. A fast, exact instrument that is a pleasure to use. Final Cut Pro is the model: not daunting at first sight, with a lot of depth for people who go looking. Copy is short and relaxed, chemist to chemist, in British English and Simplified Technical English.

## Anti-references

- Legacy academic software: dense grey toolbars, tiny bitmap icons, modal dialogs everywhere (ChemDraw's current look).
- Generic startup templates: Geist or Inter, the shadcn look, gradient heroes, glass cards.
- Anything that shouts: warm accents, bounce or spring overshoot, upsell modals, sign-up gates.

## Design Principles

1. The canvas comes first. Chrome stays out of the way and never covers the drawing without reason.
2. Fast beats clever. Every common action has a key, and nothing that happens a hundred times a day animates.
3. Correct by default. The page is ACS 1996 by the book, and export matches the page exactly.
4. Simple surface, deep tools. Beginners see a short set of tools; experts find more without hunting.
5. Never worse than ChemDraw. Check each feature side by side for output, speed and number of steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- All text meets 4.5:1 contrast in light and dark chrome.
- Every control shows a visible focus ring and works from the keyboard.
- Respect reduced motion: no movement, only short fades.
- Status uses an icon plus text, never colour alone.
