# Test pictures: old documents (public domain)

Drawings from US patents granted 1976 to 1994 (US government documents; the USPTO states that patent text and
drawings are generally not subject to copyright) and from pages of the Berichte der deutschen chemischen Gesellschaft
of 1891 (authors died in 1911 and 1931; public domain worldwide), as scanned. Crops were cut from the page; in some,
a neighbouring label or arrow was painted out. The expected structure was read from the picture atom by atom and
checked against the compound the document names. Structures with R groups (Markush structures) are kept for finding
structures but not scored: InChI has no R group. `cases.json` holds the expected SMILES.
