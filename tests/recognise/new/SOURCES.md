# Test pictures: recent papers (CC-BY)

licences). Crops were cut from the published figure; in some, a compound number or a neighbouring fragment was
painted white. The expected structure was read from the picture atom by atom and, where the paper or PubChem
names the compound, checked against that name. `cases.json` holds the expected SMILES.
